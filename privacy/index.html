Swipe Sorter: privacy policy

Iconic Tools · last updated 19 October 2026

Swipe Sorter, by Iconic Tools, sorts files on your own computer. It does not collect, send or sell personal information.

What stays on your computer

Your answers, progress, history, settings and the list of files you sorted are saved only on your computer, in the app's own folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Iconic Tools, or beside a portable copy) and in your Windows user registry. Nothing is written into the folders you sort except the changes you apply.

Microsoft Store copy

The app asks Windows whether your copy is in its trial or bought. Microsoft handles purchases and trials under its own privacy statement. The app itself sends nothing.

Copies bought on Gumroad or itch.io

When you enter a licence key, the app sends it once to our activation server together with a code made from your computer's Windows ID. The code can't be turned back into the ID and contains no names, files or other personal details. The server asks the shop whether the key is valid and sends back a signed answer that only works on that computer. The server stores nothing: the shop keeps its usual count of how many times the key has been activated. After that, the app never contacts the server again. Purchases themselves are handled by the shop under its own privacy policy.

No tracking

No analytics, advertising, crash reporting or telemetry.
